01 / Our mission

Every minute of
search counts.

Search missions should be limited by the mission, not the aircraft.

Limited flight endurance, battery changes and maintenance can interrupt a search when time is critical. Bombini is rethinking the small unmanned aircraft around the needs of the mission.

Our initial focus is defence search and rescue and emergency response in open and semi-open environments.
