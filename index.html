01 / Our mission

Every minute
of search counts.

Search and rescue teams need sustained aerial coverage, but conventional small aircraft can force difficult operational trade-offs.

Limited endurance, battery changes and maintenance can interrupt a search when time is critical. Bombini Technologies is rethinking the small unmanned aircraft around the needs of the mission, rather than asking operators to adapt the mission to the aircraft.

Our initial focus is defence search and rescue and emergency response in open and semi-open environments.
